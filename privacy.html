Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

HydrationCalc.net does not collect personal data through our tools. All calculations run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No weight, activity level, or health information is transmitted to our servers.

2. Cookies & Analytics

We may use basic analytics to understand which pages are visited most often. These analytics do not identify you personally. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

We may display advertisements through third-party networks. These partners may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

4. Data Security

Because we do not store your health or personal data, there is no database of user information to breach. Your tool inputs exist only in your browser session.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@hydrationcalc.net.
