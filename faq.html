Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common hydration questions. For deeper dives, check the blog or tools.

How much water should I drink per day?

It depends on your body weight, climate, and activity level. A general baseline is 30–35 mL per kg of body weight. Use our Daily Water Intake Calculator for a personalized goal.

Does coffee dehydrate you?

No. Coffee has a Beverage Hydration Index of about 0.98, meaning it hydrates almost as well as water. The diuretic effect of moderate caffeine is small and does not cause net fluid loss.

What is the Beverage Hydration Index?

The BHI measures how much fluid your body retains from a drink compared to plain water. Milk scores 1.5, sports drinks 0.95–1.0, and beer 0.79. Try our Beverage Hydration Index Tool.

How do I know if I am a salty sweater?

Signs include white salt stains on clothes, stinging eyes when you sweat, frequent cramping, and salt cravings after exercise. You can also do a sweat sodium patch test. Our Exercise Sweat Loss Estimator can help.

Is the 8-glasses-a-day rule accurate?

No. The "8 glasses" rule is a misinterpretation of a 1945 recommendation that included water from food. Your actual needs depend on weight, climate, and activity.

Do your tools store my personal data?

No. All calculations run entirely in your browser. We never see your weight, activity level, or health information.
