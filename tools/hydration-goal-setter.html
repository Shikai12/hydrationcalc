How It Works

Enter your daily fluid goal and log your intake each day. The streak counter tracks consecutive days you hit your goal. The weekly average smooths out day-to-day variation. The chart shows your last 14 days so you can spot patterns.

Tip: If you're consistently under your goal, lower it by 10% for a week. Build the habit first, then raise it. Perfection is not the goal — consistency is.
